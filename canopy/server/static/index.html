            Fetched

            Open-access PDFs, already downloaded and readable, plus any you supplied. Untick one to leave it out of the review; it stays on this page with its reason.

              Behind a paywall

              Canopy will not download these. Open one; if you have access, upload the PDF and it joins the review. Skip the rest — the review will say they were unavailable.

                Wanted, not answered

                The screener wanted these and the search stopped before it tried for an open copy. Nobody checked a publisher, so nobody may call them paywalled — upload one if you have it, or search again.

                  Unsure

                  The screener could not tell from the title and abstract alone. Nothing was decided for you: tick any you want in the review.

                    Excluded

                    Each one with the reason it was ruled out. Tick any the screener got wrong.

                      Not screened

                      Nobody read these — there was no model, or the search stopped first. They are listed rather than forgotten.
